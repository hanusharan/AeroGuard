Figure 6(a), printed report page 21 (PDF zero-index page 24; scanned plot):
NACA 2412 airfoil section; WSU wind tunnel tests at Re_c = 2.2e6, M = 0.13; comparison curves from the report's Reference 9 at Re_c = 3.1e6 and 5.7e6.
The lift coefficient is section lift / (q_inf c). Four series:
  circles: WSU with grit, Re 2.2e6
  squares: WSU clean, Re 2.2e6
  triangles: clean comparison from Reference 9
  diamonds: NACA grit comparison from Reference 9
The NASA report bibliography identifies Reference 9 as Abbott & von Doenhoff, Theory of Wing Sections (Dover, 1959). These are reproduced comparison curves, not new WSU measurements.
Axes visually read from source plot: alpha approximately -8 to 20 deg; cl approximately -0.2 to 1.6.
NASA report describes transition strips for the WSU series: 2.5 mm wide #80 carborundum grit at x/c=0.05 on upper and lower surfaces. The report notes clean WSU and clean Reference 9 maximum lift coefficients differ by about 0.15, reason unknown; grit conditions also differ in grit size and coverage.
